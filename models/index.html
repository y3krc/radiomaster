Install a model

  1. Grab the .yml for your radio from models/<controller>/.
  2. Copy it into the card's BACKUP/.
  3. On the radio, restore it from the model-select menu — safer than dropping straight into MODELS/.

Models are controller-specific (switch map, channel setup) — they won't map cleanly to other RadioMaster radios. And they're bind-phrase-free: ELRS keeps bind data in the module flash, not the model file, so you'll bind your own receivers.
